Strategic Brief

The thinking behind this week

The Goal This Week

Pre-launch authority building. We're 12 weeks out from the August 15 Line Eraser Serum — too far for hard sell, exactly right for credibility-building. This week we establish Melissa as a skincare authority while she's still primarily known for makeup. We do it through founder voice, not product push.

Why This CA$H Distribution

Heavy on Authority + Solution (2A / 1S / 1H / 1C). For a sales/launch lead-up week, the framework calls for Authority weighting — but at 12 weeks out we still need one Connection piece to humanize the founder before the launch arc kicks in. Cutting the second Connection slot in favor of a How-To keeps the week useful for save-and-share growth, which compounds over the next 90 days into launch reach.

What I'm Seeing in Your Niche

Founder-to-camera ingredient call-outs are over-indexing right now. Jones Road's "what's actually in your foundation" reel hit 400K+ in 15 seconds — single shot, no edits, just a founder telling the truth. We're modeling Monday's pattern off that. Bobbi Brown's age-positive carousel is showing that older-founder POV content is converting in clean luxury beauty — that informs Tuesday. Yse Beauty's textured ASMR application reel is driving product clicks with no audio talking — that's Thursday's blueprint.

The Wedge

Nobody in clean luxury beauty is naming why traditional foundation feels different on mature skin. Melissa has the formulation receipts to own this conversation. Award-winning foundation = earned authority to make the claim. Wednesday's Solution post plants this flag.

Last Week's Read

The brush care reel underperformed because it was educational without a story. We do not repeat that this week. Every piece this week has founder-as-character at its center. The lip plumper DM flood and foundation reorder rate confirm: when she's on camera, audience moves.

What We're Building Toward

Friday's podcast drop ("why I left mass beauty") gets cross-pollinated all week. Tuesday begins the seeding. Friday teases the serum without naming it — anyone who hears the podcast Friday will encounter the tease post the same day and feel the puzzle pieces clicking. That's the launch arc kickoff.

Hook (On-Screen Text)
Primary

Foundation lasts twice as long if you do this first.

Secondary

The 30-second step that doubles your foundation's wear.

Caption
Foundation lasts twice as long if you do this first. There is a 30-second step almost no one teaches, and it is the difference between foundation that wears beautifully and foundation that breaks down by lunch. Before foundation: press a hydrating mist into your skin with clean fingertips. Not spray-and-go. Press. Then wait 60 seconds. What you're doing is giving your skin's surface enough moisture for the foundation to bind to instead of cling to. Foundation on dehydrated skin grabs at dry patches, oxidizes faster, and settles into lines. Foundation on properly prepped skin moves with you for the entire day. The brush you use second matters almost as much. Synthetic fibers absorb your formula and waste it. Ours are crafted in Italy from a generations-old atelier — they distribute pigment without drinking it. Press. Wait. Apply. Done. Comment ROUTINE and I'll send you the full 5-step founder routine I use every morning. Mist and brush both linked in my bio. —Melissa
